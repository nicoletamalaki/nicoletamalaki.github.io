Instructional design & learning experience

I design learning around the people experiencing it.

Instructional Designer creating meaningful learning experiences that respond to who learners actually are—their knowledge, confidence, experience, and individual needs—so learning doesn't just happen, but lasts.

My background in education taught me that no two learners experience learning in quite the same way. Today, I bring that understanding into instructional design, combining evidence-based learning theory, thoughtful technology, and iterative design to create experiences that respect learner differences while helping people build confidence, think independently, and apply what they learn.
