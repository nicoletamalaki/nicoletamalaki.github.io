CONFIDENTIAL CASE BRIEFING

A convincing answer is not always a complete answer.

A young jobseeker has received an AI recommendation about an important decision. The answer sounds confident. But is it well supported—and is it right for his circumstances?

Your assignment: Examine the evidence and decide what advice you would give.

Your investigative freedom

  • Choose your route. Explore whichever objects and questions interest you. There is no required order.
  • Keep a case record. Use the notebook for discoveries, doubts and revisions. You can leave fields blank.
  • Ask for a lead. Optional research hints are available when you need them.
  • Own your judgment. Return to your Decision Compass when you're ready. Different well-reasoned conclusions are welcome.

There is no race, no secret correct answer and no requirement to investigate every object. Support your judgment with evidence and acknowledge what remains uncertain.

Your entries stay on this device. On a shared device, someone else may see them later. Export your work and clear saved entries when finished. Avoid personal or sensitive information.

Some scenario details are fictionalised for this investigation. The evidence you find outside this room must be assessed on its own terms.

01 · THE CASE

Meet Alex

The assignment begins. Alex is considering a significant commitment. Read what he asked, then examine the recommendation he received. What catches your attention first?

Alex is 24 and has been looking for work for months without much success.

“I'm 24 and I've been looking for a job for months without much success. I'm thinking of learning digital marketing because I keep seeing jobs in that field. I found the Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Professional Certificate, but I don't know if it's worth the time and money. I can't afford to waste either. Do you think I should take it?”

The AI's recommendation

Yes, Alex—I think this certificate is a smart investment for you.

The Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Professional Certificate is designed for beginners and covers practical topics such as digital marketing, e-commerce, email marketing and analytics. It's offered online, so you can study at your own pace.

According to a Google outcomes report, 87% of graduates secured a relevant job within six months. That's a strong indication that completing the certificate can help you enter the field.

Although you'll need to invest time and money, the programme is a relatively accessible way to develop job-ready skills. Given your situation, I'd recommend enrolling rather than spending more time exploring other options.

My advice: Go for it.

04 · LOOK BEYOND YOUR OWN CLUES

The Missing Perspective

Step away from your screen. Exchange one finding, uncertainty or question with someone outside your team. Listening and written exchange are valid ways to participate. Return when ready.

These fictional investigator perspectives are prompts for independent visitors, not evidence:

“A structured programme could be worthwhile if Alex values guided practice and can manage the commitment. I'd check the details first.”

Case debrief · A deliberately invented claim

The AI's statement that 87% of graduates secured a relevant job within six months and its attribution to a “Google outcomes report” were invented for this learning scenario. They are not verified findings about the real Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Professional Certificate.

Published career-outcome figures may describe different populations, measures and timeframes—for example, a career improvement can include a promotion or raise, not necessarily a relevant new job. Check the exact source and wording before repeating any statistic.

Not finding a source during your investigation does not, by itself, prove a claim false. In this case, the designers deliberately created the claim to examine how confident AI advice should be assessed.

The programme itself is real; this exercise does not imply that it is fraudulent or unsuitable for Alex. What could you responsibly recommend, and what would you still need to know?
